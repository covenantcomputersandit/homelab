reconstructed from memory or old notes. Findings become roadmap items, and
the full documentation trail is kept privately.

## What's working well

- **Layered monitoring with push alerting,** covering disks, the array,
  the system, the network and power.
- **Every change is documented** in an append-only, dated log for each
  machine and project.
- **Deploys are verified and reversible.** Every deploy follows the same
  steps: verify the hash, compare against what's live, back up, install,
  check.
- **Agents work behind approval gates,** enforced both by policy and by
  each session's safety checks.
